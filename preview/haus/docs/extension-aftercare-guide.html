01 · The first few days

Settling in — what's normal, what's not

For the first few days after your install, it's completely normal for your scalp to feel a little tight, tender, or even itchy as it gets used to carrying the beads. Everyone's different — some feel nothing at all.

✓ Totally normal

  • Mild tightness or tenderness that eases within a few days as your scalp adjusts.
  • A little itching while your scalp gets used to the extensions sitting close to the skin.
  • Being extra aware of the weight and the bead area — that fades fast, too.

⚑ Give it a few days

  • Resist tight, high, slicked-back styles while everything settles.
  • Be gentle around the bead rows — no pulling or picking.
  • If tightness is keeping you up at night, that's a sign a bead may be too snug — tell us.
⚠️

Reach out right away if…

It lasts longer than two weeks, becomes painful, or you notice redness or bumps at the beads. That's not something to wait out — message us and we'll help.

02 · Brushing

Daily brushing is non-negotiable

It's the single simplest habit that keeps everything else working — it prevents matting and tangling and keeps your natural hair healthy underneath.

Use the right brush

An extension-friendly brush only — a loop brush, wet brush, or soft bristle.

Start at the ends, work up

Always brush the ends first and move upward, so you never pull tension straight onto the beads.

Support the root

Hold your hair near the root while you brush to take the strain off the beads.

Run fingers through the rows daily

This clears shed hair before it can tangle and knot into the beads.

💜

The one habit to never skip

Brush every day — ideally morning and night. Skipped brushing is the number-one cause of matting at the root, and it's completely preventable.

04 · Moisture = life

The one thing that decides how long they last

Your extensions don't get your scalp's natural oils, so it's on you to replace that hydration. Without it, they get dry, frizzy, and break faster.

Your moisture routine

  • Daily: a light oil or serum on the ends.
  • Every wash (and in between): a leave-in conditioner spray.
  • Weekly: a deep hydrating mask, mids to ends.

Protect your water, too

  • Install a shower filter — especially for blondes — to stop the brassiness and dryness that hard water and chlorine cause.
  • Soft, filtered water keeps colour truer and hair softer for longer.

“Moisture is probably the single most important thing for longevity — so if you think you've moisturised enough… do it again.”

Say it with us
05 · Styling & sleeping

Style freely, sleep smart

You can absolutely heat-style — the rules are simply about protecting the bond and never going to bed with hair that can tangle.

✓ Styling

  • Curl, straighten or blow-dry — always with a heat protectant.
  • Keep hot tools away from the beads. Only blow dryers are safe near the roots.
  • Oil on the ends only, to keep hair soft and shiny (never at the roots).

Sleeping

  • Always braid or tie a loose pony before bed.
  • Never sleep with wet hair — it's a fast track to tangling and matting.
  • Use a silk bonnet or pillowcase to keep frizz and tangles down.
✨

Healthy styling = longer-lasting hair

Gentle heat, a good protectant, and a loose braid on silk at night add up to weeks of extra wear and far fewer tangles.

06 · Wavy & curly care

Textured sets need a little extra TLC

Wavy and curly hair is naturally drier and tangles more easily, so it thrives on moisture and gentle, hands-first handling.

🌊 Wavy hair

  • Scrunch with curl cream or leave-in to keep waves defined.
  • Detangle in the shower with conditioner, using fingers or a wide-tooth comb.
  • Air dry or diffuse on low. Never brush when dry unless you want them to fluff out.
  • Limit heat — too much flattens the wave pattern.
  • Leave-in conditioner, generously. The more moisture, the better.

💜 Curly hair

  • Curl cream, spray or leave-in every one to two days.
  • Detangle gently when damp with fingers or a wide-tooth comb — don't brush.
  • Wrap in silk or satin at night to preserve the curls.
  • Use a curl-refresher spray between washes to revive definition.
💜

A note on timing

Because textured hair is drier and can tangle more easily, wavy and curly clients usually need maintenance a little sooner — closer to 6–7 weeks.

07 · Vacations, sun & water

Sun, salt, chlorine & sunscreen are the enemies

Holidays are the fastest way to age a set if you're not careful. A little prep keeps your colour and condition intact.

✓ Before & after the water

  • Wear a hat for UV protection.
  • Coat with leave-in conditioner before swimming — it acts as a barrier.
  • Wash after swimming to get chlorine and salt out, then blow-dry.

⚠ The sunscreen trap

  • Mineral sunscreen only. Chemical sunscreens (avobenzone, oxybenzone and similar) can stain extensions pink or orange.
  • Watch body sprays and tanning products near your hairline, too.
☀️

Honestly? Keep them out of the water

If you can, your best bet is to keep your extensions out of the pool and ocean altogether. When you can't, prep and rinse — but dry hair is happy hair.

08 · Discolouration — the big one

Why extensions change colour — and how to stop it

This is one of the most common problems clients face. Extensions discolour faster than natural hair because of their porosity — and blondes especially should watch for brassy, peachy, pink or green tones.

What causes it

Chlorine Hard water — iron, copper, minerals UV exposure Chemical sunscreens & oils Perfume & body sprays Tinted lotions & products

Prevention & care

✓ Everyday habits

  • Rinse after chlorine or salt water.
  • Stick to mineral sunscreen only.
  • Install or use a shower filter.
  • Use Malibu C products to prevent and correct mineral buildup.

⚑ Use with care

  • Purple shampoo — sparingly only. It grabs FAST; dilute it and always strand-test first.
  • Clarify gently once in a while to reset product and mineral buildup — then follow with a hydrating mask.

10 · Products

Not all hair products are extension-safe

The wrong ingredients cause slipping, buildup, dryness or discolouration. When in doubt, keep it simple and gentle.

✕ Avoid products with

  • Sulfates — strip extensions and dry them out.
  • Parabens — harsh and drying.
  • Heavy silicones or waxes — coat the hair, cause buildup, make it dull, and cause slippage.
  • Proteins or bond builders — make extensions brittle.
  • Hidden alcohols — dryness and breakage.
💜

Don't stress about finding the right stuff

We stock only what works — everything you need is at the salon, already tested and approved for extensions. If you're ever unsure about a product, just ask us before you use it.

11 · Maintenance

The 6–8 week cycle

Your hair keeps growing, so the beads travel down and need to be repositioned. Coming in on schedule is what keeps your natural hair healthy.

How to come to your appointment

  • Arrive with clean, dry, straight hair — or styled in your natural texture if you're wavy or curly.
  • Your natural hair sheds 50–150 hairs a day, which stay trapped in the beads. You'll see that shed come out during removal — totally normal, not breakage.

✕ Two hard rules

  • Never remove your own extensions. It will cause damage if it's not done properly.
  • MRIs: extensions must be removed beforehand — the beads are metal and can't go in the machine. Tell us as soon as you know your date.
12 · Do's & don'ts

The golden rules, at a glance

Screenshot this one. Simple habits = long-lasting, flawless extensions.

✓ Do

  • Brush daily and detangle gently.
  • Hydrate mids to ends with light oils and leave-ins.
  • Deep condition weekly.
  • Always use a heat protectant.
  • Sleep in a loose braid or ponytail, on silk.

✕ Don't

  • No conditioner or masks near the beads.
  • No protein or bond-building products — too harsh.
  • Don't rough-towel dry or sleep with wet roots.
  • Don't use too high a heat on your extensions.
  • Avoid cheap drugstore products with sulfates, silicones or hidden alcohols.
13 · Common concerns

“Is this normal?” — usually, yes

A quick reference for the things clients ask about most. When something's tagged watch, it's worth a message if it doesn't settle.

NormalSlipped extensions
Losing 2–12 between visits is normal. Keep any that slip and bring them to maintenance — we'll reapply them.
WatchTightness or itchiness
Should fade within a week. If it doesn't, reach out — a bead may need adjusting.
NormalWhite bulbs at the beads
Totally normal! These are your natural shed hairs (telogen bulbs) trapped by the beads until removal. Not breakage.
WatchMatting
Happens if brushing is skipped or you sleep with wet hair. Prevent it with daily detangling and always blow-drying the bead area.
NormalShedding from the hair itself
Some shedding is normal — the hair isn't attached to a scalp anymore. As long as it isn't excessive clumps, it's fine.
NormalBeads showing
Can peek through if your hair shifts, grows out, or you wear high or tight styles. Usually fixed at maintenance or by adjusting how you style it.
NormalBubbling
The top row can feel bumpy or lifted from pulling hair up a certain way, or from how it's brushed. Easily fixed at maintenance.
NormalFading colour
Factory colour fades over time with washing, heat, UV and product use — normal wear. A toning or colour-refresh service brings it back.

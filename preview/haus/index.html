Our signature

Nano bead extensions

Our most requested method. Placed strand by strand, each bead about the size of a grain of rice. Lightweight, discreet, glue-free and heat-free... one of the gentlest methods on the market, designed with fine and fragile hair in mind.

One method. Perfected.

In Sarasota, we do one thing.

Nano beads. Nothing else. No wefts, no tape, no shortcuts — just the gentlest extension method on the market, installed by hands trained to the standard that made us Barrie's best. A world-class craft, finally home in Florida.

Explore our services and pricing

Genius Wefts

Hidden beaded wefts. A beautiful option for clients with normal to thick hair who prefer a row method. Hidden beads keep the weft sitting flat while creating maximum fullness and length with fewer attachment points.

Custom K-Tips

Individual strands fused with a keratin bond with no beads. Our lightest, most flexible attachment, reserved for the finest and most fragile hair that other methods can't safely carry.

Custom color

Whether you wear extensions or not, beautiful color starts with a personalized approach. We create dimensional, natural, looking color that is customized to your hair, your goals, and your lifestyle.

The Healthy Hair Promise

Four promises we can make to every client.

  1. We will never recommend more hair than your natural hair can safely support.

What we do

Extensions, matched to your hair — not the other way around.

Nano bead extensions

Our signature. The smallest attachment system there is — a tiny ring, no glue, no heat, no chemicals — placed strand by strand so it lies flat and moves like your own hair. Ideal for fine and thin hair.

Custom K-Tips & Genius Weft

For the most fragile hair types we offer custom K-Tips, and Genius Weft for density that wants a seamless row. The consultation decides the method — your hair sets the rules.

Before you book

Hair extensions in Barrie — the questions we hear most.

How much do hair extensions cost in Barrie?

Every head is quoted at your free consultation, because the number of bundles depends on your length, density and the look you are after. Nano bead installs start at $230 per bundle and maintenance starts at $145. If you already have extension hair, we assess it first and install it for $85 a bundle. The full menu is on the service menu.

How often do I need a maintenance appointment?

Every six to eight weeks. Your own hair grows about a centimetre a month, so the beads travel down and have to be repositioned. A maintenance appointment is a full removal, a cleanse and a refit — not a quick push-up — which is what keeps the hair underneath healthy.

Will nano bead extensions damage my hair?

Not when they are fitted, maintained and cared for properly. Nano beads use no glue, no heat and no chemicals — the bond is a tiny bead closed around a few of your own strands. The real risks are skipping maintenance and wearing more hair than your own can carry, which is exactly what the consultation is for. Removal is a salon job at $95 — never a kitchen-table one.

How long does the extension hair last?

The hair itself is reusable for roughly six to twelve months with regular maintenance and the right aftercare, so after the first install you are usually paying for the refit rather than a whole new set. How to wash it, sleep in it and keep it that way is on the aftercare page.

Do you take clients from outside Barrie?

Yes — guests travel in from Innisfil, Angus, Orillia, Alliston, Wasaga Beach and across Simcoe County. The studio is at 480 Mapleton Ave, Barrie, open Monday to Friday 10:00 to 4:00 and Saturday 10:00 to 3:00, by appointment.

Our specialty

Nano bead extensions

This is what we're known for. Applied strand by strand, using tiny beads about the size of a grain of rice, nano extensions are our preferred method for creating beautiful, natural, looking hair without glue, heat, or bulky attachment points. Designed with healthy hair in mind, they're lightweight, comfortable, and especially well suited for fine to medium hair.

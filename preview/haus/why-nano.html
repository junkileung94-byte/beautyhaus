What are nano beads?

The tiniest way to add hair — explained.

A nano bead is a tiny ring — about the size of a grain of rice — that holds a fine strand of extension hair onto a few of your own. No glue, no heat, no chemicals. It's the smallest bead system there is, which is exactly why it suits fine, thin and fragile hair.

Placed strand by strand and tucked at the root, it lies flat, moves like your own hair, and comes out as gently as it goes in.

Made for thin & fragile hair

If you've been turned away, start here.

Nano is genuinely made for very thin, over-processed or shedding hair. That hair is fragile — which is exactly why it has to be fitted properly, and why we assess yours honestly first.

So many fine-haired women have been let down before: beads too big, extensions too heavy. Nano is the gentle option that finally fits — fullness now, while your own hair stays safe to grow long and strong.

The honest answers

What you're really wondering.

What exactly are nano bead extensions?

A fine nano-tip strand, held to a few of your own strands by a tiny nano bead — the smallest bead system there is, which is exactly why it suits finer hair.

How long do they last?

Over a year with good hair, refreshed every 6–10 weeks at a maintenance visit — a gentle re-tighten as your own hair grows out, never a full new set each time.

Are they damaging?

Not when they're fitted by a trained hand. No glue, no heat — the least damaging permanent method there is. Removal needs no acetone or alcohol, and each strand is featherlight (about a gram).

Are they really right for thin hair?

That's who they were made for. If you've been told you're "not a candidate," or found other methods too heavy, this is the one most likely to change that answer — fitted to what your hair can safely carry.

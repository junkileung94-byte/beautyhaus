What are nano beads?

Tiny beads. Individual strands.

Nano bead extensions are installed one strand at a time using a tiny metal bead, about the size of a grain of rice.

There is no glue, tape, heat, or sewing. Because every strand is placed individually, the color, density, and placement can be customized throughout your hair.

Why women choose nano

Six reasons they feel different.

01

Lightweight by design

Small, individual strands help distribute the weight throughout the head instead of concentrating it in one bulky attachment.

02

Easy to hide

Tiny beads sit, discreetly near the root, making ponytails, buns, and half up styles easier to wear.

03

Natural movement

Because because the hair is installed straight by strand, it moves more like your own hair without the stiffness of a large weft.

04

Custom color blending

Multiple shades can be blended throughout your set for a detailed colour match that looks natural from root to end.

05

Reusable hair

With proper care, the same extension hair can be removed and reinstalled throughout multiple maintenance appointments.

06

Built around your hair

Your length, fullness, placement, and number of strands are customized to your natural density and goals.

The honest answers

What you're really wondering.

Will nano beads damage my hair?

No permanent extension method is completely risk free. When nano beads are properly installed, maintained, and worn by the right candidate, they are considered one of the gentler permanent methods. We will never add more weight than your natural hair can comfortably support.

Do they hurt?

They should not hurt. You may notice some tenderness or awareness during the first few days while your scalp adjusts, but the beads should never feel painfully tight or pull on your natural hair..

How long does the extension hair last?

With proper care, the extension hair typically lasts 9 to 18 months and can be reused to multiple maintenance appointments. Bloner shades usually fall closer to the lower end of that range because lighter hair has undergone more processing. Texture, heat use, home care, and maintenance habits can also affect the lifespan.

Are nanos right for fine hair?

They can be a great option for fine to normal density hair because the strands are so small and the placement can be customized. However, active shedding, weakened roots, or very fragile hair may mean extensions are not the right choice at that time.

What are nano beads?

The gentlest way to wear hair extensions.

A nano bead is a tiny ring — about the size of a grain of rice — that holds a fine strand of extension hair onto a few strands of your own. No glue, no tape, no heat, no chemicals. It's the smallest attachment system in the industry, which is exactly why it works for the fine and thin hair that other methods punish.

Placed strand by strand and tucked at the root, nano beads lie flat, move like your own hair, and come out as gently as they went in. Curious about the method? Read why women choose nano beads or the complete nano extensions guide.
